# Taura Laboratory

## Overview

Taura laboratory is a research laboratory in the [Department of Information and Communication Engineering](https://www.i.u-tokyo.ac.jp/edu/course/ice/index_e.shtml) of the [Graduate School of Information Science and Technology](https://www.i.u-tokyo.ac.jp/index_e.shtml), [University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/index_e.html).

Our main expertise is on parallel computing, distributed computing, system software, and programming languages.
We are especially interested in high level parallel processing software, which makes the power of high
performance computers much more accessible to domain scientists.
